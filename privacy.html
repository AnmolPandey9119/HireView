Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

HireView ("HireView", "we", "us", or "our") provides AI-powered mock interview practice. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and services (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using HireView, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: we collect the account details and interview data needed to run mock interviews and give you feedback, we never sell your personal data, and your camera/microphone are only used live during a session — we do not record or store interview video or audio.

1.Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. We send a one-time verification code (OTP) to your email to confirm it's really you, and use the same mechanism for password resets. If you add a profile picture, we store that too.

Interview data. When you take a mock interview, we collect and store:

  • The role, language, and settings you choose for the session
  • The questions asked and your spoken/typed responses (converted to text via your browser's speech recognition)
  • AI-generated feedback and scores for your session, and your interview history

Camera, microphone & proctoring signals. With your permission, we access your camera and microphone during a live interview so the AI interviewer can see and hear you and so we can generate feedback on your delivery. Alongside this, to keep practice sessions honest, we detect (locally, in your browser):

  • Whether you switch tabs or the browser window loses focus during a session
  • Whether a face is consistently visible, or multiple faces appear, on camera

Face detection runs client-side and is used only to generate a signal (e.g. "no face detected"); we do not record, store, or transmit your raw video or audio stream to our servers. Only the resulting text-based signals (such as counts of tab switches or face-detection flags) are saved with your interview record.

Technical information. We may automatically collect limited technical data such as browser type, device information, and general usage logs (e.g. timestamps, error logs) to keep the Service secure and working correctly.

2.How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your identity via OTP
  • To run mock interview sessions and generate AI feedback on your answers
  • To show you your interview history and track your progress over time
  • To detect and discourage cheating during practice sessions
  • To send you transactional emails (OTP codes, password resets, account notices)
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and performance of the Service
  • To improve HireView's features based on aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns

We do not use your interview answers or personal data to train third-party AI models, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

We only share data with service providers who help us run HireView, under obligations to protect it:

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of HireView or our users, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — in which case we'll notify you.

4.Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential cookies/local storage to keep you logged in and remember your session and preferences (e.g. selected interview language). We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

5.Data Retention

We retain your account information and interview history for as long as your account is active, so you can track your progress over time. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7). We do not retain raw camera or microphone streams at all — these exist only transiently in your browser during a live session.

6.Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access controls on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to the best of our ability.

7.Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction: You can view and update your profile information from your account settings.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us (below).
  • Camera/microphone: You can deny or revoke camera/microphone permissions in your browser at any time; note that this may prevent you from taking a live interview.
  • Marketing emails: We currently only send transactional emails (OTP, account/security notices), not marketing emails.

If you are located in a region with specific data protection laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR or India's DPDP Act), you may have additional rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8.Children's Privacy

HireView is intended for job seekers and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9.International Users

HireView is built primarily for job seekers in India, and your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer and processing.

10.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you via email or an in-app notice.

11.Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise any of your rights, contact us.
