Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This policy explains when you're eligible for a refund on a HireView plan purchase, and how to request one. It applies to the Weekly (₹99) and Monthly (₹299) plans described on our pricing page.

In short: if something goes wrong with your payment or you were charged in error, we'll refund you in full. Because a plan unlocks unlimited interview access immediately, refunds for "change of mind" after you've started using a plan are limited — see below.

1.No Auto-Renewal

HireView plans are one-time payments for a fixed period (7 or 30 days). We do not store your card/UPI details for auto-billing, and you will never be charged again automatically after a plan expires. There is nothing to "cancel" in the subscription-billing sense — simply don't purchase another plan when the current one ends.

2.When You're Eligible for a Full Refund

  • Duplicate or failed payment: if you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or charged but your plan was never activated due to a technical error on our end.
  • Unauthorized transaction: if a payment was made from your account without your authorization (subject to standard verification).
  • Service not delivered: if you purchased a plan and were unable to access any interviews under it due to a platform-side issue that we could not resolve within a reasonable time.

In these cases, contact us within 7 days of the transaction and we'll investigate and refund you in full if eligible.

3.Change-of-Mind Refunds

Because a plan purchase unlocks unlimited interview access immediately, we offer a limited change-of-mind window:

  • You must initiate your refund request within 72 hours of purchase. Requests made after 72 hours will not be considered, regardless of usage.
  • Within that 72-hour window, you're eligible for a full refund only if you have not started any interview under that plan. If you've already started at least one interview, the purchase is non-refundable even if it's within 72 hours.
Not eligible for refund: requests made after 72 hours of purchase, plans that have been used (even partially, even within 72 hours), and the free trial (since it costs nothing).

4.How to Request a Refund

Contact us with:

  • The email address on your HireView account
  • The Razorpay payment ID or the approximate date/amount of the transaction
  • A brief reason for the refund request

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
